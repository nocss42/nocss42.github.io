What is this about?

Myself and a friend who goes by ud2, started cracking mods for a game called People Playground, the reason being that Patreons were expensive and we didn't want to buy it just to play with a mod.

At first our little group was called "Havok" back when ud2 made a crack of a Kaiju mod, but we moved on from that name after 7.0, partly due to him getting doxed by someone.

The main mod we focused all our efforts on was Jujtusu Playground, we were both stupid and didn't know how to reverse anything (ud2 had experience in asm but not mono) and we spent months fumbling around with their mod protection, from shit as simple as IsLicensed() to hash checks that crashed the game. Then came our first release under the havok name, playing on the name of someone elses crack of the mod "Despair Edition".
